## Phase 0 acceptance boundary

The contract preserves source symbol and metadata snapshot identity through a
Host-only round trip. Identity, explicit unit conversion, and explicit axis
permutation are the only mapping kinds. Inference, stale snapshot reuse,
unknown units, implicit axis replacement, and Host-key-only identity are
rejected. This proposal does not alter the already accepted ScientificInput or
CoefficientTensor contracts.
