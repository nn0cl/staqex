## Hardening notes for any future ADR amendment

1. Make the purity/type gate explicit in the acceptance contract: polynomial
   fusion is permitted only after normal type/dimension validation and only for
   the supported scalar carrier domain. A parser match alone is not authority.
2. Document finite numeric semantics: coefficients are currently `f64`, degree
   cap is 8, and near-zero coefficient trimming uses a tolerance. A future
   change must state whether this is an optimization-equivalence tolerance or
   merely diagnostic metadata, and must preserve fallback behavior.
3. Keep `last_algebraic_fusion` and `last_poly_fusion` as diagnostic evidence,
   not as a second semantic representation. The canonical meaning remains the
   source-derived operation.
4. Do not extend the accepted scope to division, rational powers, `when`/Call
   rewrites, symbolic coefficients, operator-matrix multiplication, trait
   specialization, or effect rows without a new reviewed acceptance contract
   and ADR.

## Conclusion

ADR-0157 is architecturally acceptable for its narrow, already-shipped
classical optimization. The WP-0063 branch should not be merged: it is a stale
and partly mixed historical snapshot. Preserve the hardening notes as future
review criteria, and retire the branch only after explicit deletion approval.
